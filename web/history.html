Version History

What's new in each release of Yearbirder.

v2.14 September 1, 2026 Latest
  • Open a hotspot's sightings straight from the map

    Clicking a dot on the Hotspot Map now opens an All Community Sightings report for that hotspot, so you can go from spotting a promising location on the map to reading what has been seen there without setting the filter yourself. Hovering a dot still shows its name, species count and last observation date. Clicking through was always meant to work and never did — the same fix revives it on the species maps too.

v2.13 August 29, 2026
  • Sort dates by day of year

    Right-click any date column header in a list and choose Sort by day of year (seasonal) to reorder the rows by where each date falls in the year, ignoring which year it was — every January date first, every December date last. It answers a different question from an ordinary date sort: not when did this happen but when in the year do I see this. Sort your Species List by First this way and your species lay themselves out in the order they arrive through the season, resident birds and early migrants at the top, late-autumn arrivals at the bottom. Available on every date column in the Species, Locations, Checklists and Find Results lists, and on the Date tab of Date Totals. While seasonal, the header reads First (seasonal) so you always know which ordering you are looking at.

  • Street maps no longer show a watermark

    The service supplying Yearbirder's street map tiles began requiring a paid key and started stamping "API KEY REQUIRED" across every map. Yearbirder now draws its street maps from Esri — the same provider that has always supplied the satellite view — so maps are clean again and the app relies on one map service instead of two. The map looks slightly different, a little warmer and softer, but works exactly as before, including the Satellite toggle, Reset and Full Screen buttons. Nothing to set up, and no key needed.

  • Fix a photo's species or location without leaving it

    The photo Enlargement view's right-click menu gains Edit species or location assignment…, which opens Manage Photos on just the photo you are looking at. When you spot a misfiled photo while browsing, you can correct it there and then instead of hunting for it in a Manage Photos window full of everything else.

  • Come back to the photo you were viewing

    Switching to another app while viewing a photo or recording full screen, then switching back, used to return you to the main window with no way to reach the full-screen view again — it was still open, just unreachable. Yearbirder now brings you back to what you were actually looking at.

  • Interface refinements

    Window and table borders now meet cleanly at their corners rather than leaving a small gap, the Preferences window gains the same thin border as every other window, and clearing the filters no longer leaves a stray highlight on whichever control you touched last.

v2.12 August 14, 2026
  • Sort in either direction

    Both browse views for photos and recordings — Browse Cards and Browse Grid — now sort in either direction. A pair of Ascending and Descending buttons sits beside the four sort options, so you can finally see your newest photos first, work up from your lowest-rated recordings, or run the taxonomy backwards. Each sort option still opens the way it always did: Rating starts with your best, everything else starts at the beginning, so nothing changes until you ask it to.

  • Spectrograms on the recording growth curve

    Hovering a point on Recordings → Recorded Species Growth Over Time now shows a spectrogram of each species you recorded for the first time that day, the way the photography chart shows thumbnails. Clicking that point opens those recordings in a Browse Grid window, ready to play — previously it listed every species you had seen that day, recorded or not.

  • My County and My Patch, always at hand

    The My County and My Patch buttons now sit at the top of the Sighting Filter whether or not you have set them. Click one before it is set and Yearbirder explains what it does and offers to take you straight to the right tab of Preferences; right-click either one to go there any time. Previously the buttons stayed hidden until configured, so there was nothing to discover.

  • Lists show which column they are sorted by

    Species, Checklists, Locations and Find Results windows now open with the sort arrow visible on the column they are already sorted by — a hint that every column header is clickable. The rows themselves are unchanged; the arrow simply tells you what was previously left unsaid.

  • Clearer wording when renaming media

    The Cancel button in the Rename Media window is now labelled Close, since renames are applied as they run and closing the window never undoes them.

v2.11 August 11, 2026
  • Browse your photos as a grid

    A second way to browse: Photos → Browse Grid shows the photos matching your filters as a grid of thumbnails rather than one card per row. It is built for large results — a filter returning a thousand images, or the hundred-odd photos you may have of a single species, is far quicker to scan several across at a time. Each cell shows the species name, with the full details on hover, and clicking one opens the enlargement view as before.

  • Browse your recordings as a grid

    Recordings → Browse Grid does the same for your sound recordings, laying them out as a grid of spectrograms. Every cell keeps its own Play button and scrubber, so you can audition a recording without leaving the grid, and the red cursor line tracks the sound across the cell exactly as it does on a card.

  • Two browse views, side by side

    The original browsers are unchanged but renamed Browse Cards, so each menu now offers the pair: Browse Grid for scanning many at once, Browse Cards for reading the full details of one at a time. Both views share the same four sorting options, the same Slideshow button, and the same live updates when media is deleted, removed from the catalog, or reassigned. The Photos and Recordings toolbar buttons open the grid views, and the two windows are independent — keep a grid open for scanning and a cards window alongside it.

  • Grids reflow to your window

    Widen or narrow a grid window and it re-lays its cells to fit as many columns as the space allows, without reloading a single thumbnail or re-rendering a spectrogram. Photo grids open four columns wide, recording grids three.

  • macOS app icon

    On the Mac, Yearbirder's icon now matches the size and rounded-square shape of every other app in the Dock and the ⌘-Tab switcher, instead of appearing as an oversized square beside them.

v2.10 August 7, 2026
  • Instant previews for new recordings

    The spectrogram views a recording needs are now pre-rendered when you add it to your catalog, so newly added recordings open instantly in both the browser and the enlargement view instead of rendering on first use.

  • Compact catalog and cache

    A new command tidies your media catalog and sweeps cached previews that no longer belong to it, reclaiming disk space. Removing a photo or recording from the catalog now frees its cached previews automatically.

  • Single-species filter opens the Individual window

    Asking for a species list when your Sighting Filter names just one species now opens that species' Individual window — its dates, locations, and media — instead of a one-row list.

  • Refresh camera and recording data respects your filters

    Re-reading technical data from your files now honors both the Sighting and Media filters, so you can refresh a chosen subset rather than the whole catalog, and each item keeps its rating and notes. The confirmation names the scope it acted on.

  • Choose how far back community sightings reach

    The Community Sightings Explorer has a new slider that sets how many days of eBird history the Notable Community Sightings, All Community Sightings, and Notable Sightings Map reports cover — anywhere from a single day to a full month, in place of the fixed three-day window.

  • Quicker ways around your photos and recordings

    Double-click an enlarged photo to snap it back to best fit after zooming with the scroll wheel, and press the space bar in a recording enlargement to start or pause playback wherever the keyboard focus sits. In the Media filter, setting only the top of a rating range now fills in the bottom for you.

  • Interface refinements and fixes

    Find results now show the terms you searched for, the Manage Photos and Manage Recordings windows label their confirm button "Save", and enlarged photos open at their final size instead of resizing after they appear. Windows users no longer see a flash when clicking a spectrogram, and the Yearbirder icon now appears in the main window's title bar. This release also brings a range of smaller stability and correctness fixes across the media views.

v2.06 July 22, 2026
  • Bird recordings

    Yearbirder now catalogs your bird recordings alongside your sightings and photos. Import your WAV files and the app reads their embedded recorder timestamps to match each one to a checklist and species — and a single recording can be assigned to several species at once. Add, browse, rate, filter, map, and rename your recordings just as you do your photos.

  • Spectrograms and synchronized playback

    Every recording appears as a spectrogram. Open one in the enlargement view to zoom both the frequency (kHz) and time axes and adjust the contrast, then play it back with a cursor that tracks the sound in real time so you can follow a song note by note. High-quality playback resamples to your output device's preferred rate.

  • Per-device audio calibration

    Because different audio hardware introduces different latency between what you hear and where the playback cursor sits, a new per-device calibration lets you align the two once. Yearbirder remembers the setting for each output device.

  • Recordings on maps and in charts

    Plot your recordings on an interactive map with spectrogram previews, or play them back in sequence on an animated map. The Audio Species Gallery shows your best recording per species, and new recording charts track your totals, year-to-date pace, accumulation, and family breakdown.

  • Filter recordings by audio properties

    Beyond species, region, date, and season, you can now filter recordings by rating, duration, sample rate, bit depth, channels, and recording device.

  • Redesigned photo and recording browsers

    The Browse Photos and Browse Recordings windows share a new media-card design, with instant sorting by species, date, rating, or taxonomy. The enlargement view has a redesigned details pane showing the full sighting, media, and technical information at a glance.

  • Dramatically faster media display

    A new caching system pre-renders photo thumbnails and recording spectrograms and stores them on disk, so browsers, galleries, and maps open dramatically faster — and stay fast between sessions instead of rebuilding every preview from scratch.

  • Live updates when your media changes

    Open browsers, maps, charts, and lists now update on the fly when you delete, re-tag, or edit a photo or recording — no need to close and reopen them.

  • Species List media icons, Notes search, and smarter matching

    Species lists now show photo and recording icons next to each species you've captured, so you can see your media coverage at a glance. Media Notes are now searchable, and photo-to-species matching includes a banding-code fallback for more reliable results.

  • Explorer region tree-picker

    The Community Sightings Explorer now uses a drill-down region tree — country to state to county — in place of the cascading dropdowns, making it easier to navigate to any region in the world.

  • Windows display fixes

    Resolved Windows-only issues that could produce stretched or mis-rendered text and brief flashes during startup.

  • Numerous interface improvements and bug fixes

    This release also brings many smaller refinements across the app — polished layouts and typography, smoother window behavior, and a wide range of stability and correctness fixes.

v1.492 May 18, 2026
  • Windows: eBird API fixed

    Resolved a Windows-only issue where all Community features (Community Sightings Explorer, Notable and All Community Sightings, Regional Taxonomy, Hotspot Map) silently failed to contact the eBird API in the installer build due to SSL certificate handling in the bundled runtime.

  • Windows: map tile backgrounds fixed

    Resolved a Windows-only issue where sightings maps, hotspot maps, animated maps, and choropleth maps displayed dots and region outlines correctly but showed a plain grey background instead of the map tile layer.

v1.49 May 16, 2026
  • Community menu

    A new Community menu consolidates the reports that contact eBird's servers: Regional Taxonomy, Notable Community Sightings (Past 3 days), All Community Sightings (Past 3 days), Hotspot Map, and Community Sightings Explorer. All community features require a free eBird API key (set in Preferences).

  • Hotspot Map

    A new Community → Hotspot Map command displays all public eBird hotspots in the selected region as bubble markers, with each bubble sized by checklist count. Also accessible from the Community Sightings Explorer. Requires an eBird API key.

  • Regional Taxonomy improvements

    The former "Regional Species" report has been renamed Regional Taxonomy and moved to Community → Regional Taxonomy. When a specific eBird location is selected in the Sighting Filter, the report now queries that exact location's species list instead of falling back to the county level. Private eBird locations are automatically detected and labelled "(Personal Location)" in the report header.

  • "(Past 3 days)" labelling

    The Notable Community Sightings and All Community Sightings menu items and their corresponding buttons in the Community Sightings Explorer now include "(Past 3 days)" to make the reporting window explicit.

  • Single-location All Community Sightings

    When a specific eBird location is selected, the All Community Sightings report now shows All-time List and Map buttons in the header, opening the Regional Taxonomy and Hotspot Map respectively for that location — even when no checklists have been submitted in the past three days.

  • Community Sightings Explorer

    The Explorer now includes a Hotspot Map button alongside the existing Notable and All Community Sightings buttons.

  • Photo Filter keyboard shortcut

    Press Cmd-P (Mac) or Ctrl-P (Windows) to show or hide the Photo Filter panel without using the mouse.

  • Badge display fixes

    Corrected several bugs where Life, State, County, and Year badges on the Notable Community Sightings and All Community Sightings reports showed incorrect results, including cases where the wrong region's data was used to evaluate firsts.

v1.48 May 2026
  • Community Sightings Explorer

    A new Reports → Community Sightings Explorer… window lets you browse Notable and All Community Sightings for any region in the world — independently of the Sighting Filter. Pick a country from a live eBird API list (United States and Canada sorted to the top), then optionally narrow to a state/province and county. Click Notable Community Sightings or All Community Sightings to open the corresponding report for that region. Requires a free eBird API key.

  • Clickable species names in community reports

    In both Notable Community Sightings and All Community Sightings, species common names are now clickable blue links. Clicking a name opens an Individual Species window showing your full sighting history, location breakdowns, monthly patterns, and photo thumbnails for that species. If the species is not in your own data, clicking it does nothing. Hybrid species remain non-clickable.

  • Lifer sort fixed in All Community Sightings

    Life-bird species now appear in correct taxonomic position in the All Community Sightings report. Previously, species you had never recorded anywhere sorted to the bottom of the list instead of their correct taxonomic position.

  • Removed redundant Reports count

    The All Community Sightings header no longer shows a separate Reports count. Since the eBird API returns one report per species, that number always equalled the species count and provided no additional information.

v1.47 May 2026
  • Notable Community Sightings

    Community → Notable Community Sightings (Past 3 days) queries the eBird API for species flagged as notable in the selected region over the past three days, sorted taxonomically. Colour-coded badge bubbles mark Life, State, County, and Year firsts relative to your own data. Click + to expand each species' checklist entries; duplicate entries (same location, time, and observer) are removed automatically. Hybrid species appear in grey without badges. Requires an eBird API key.

  • All Community Sightings

    Community → All Community Sightings (Past 3 days) shows every species reported in the selected region over the past three days, one row per species showing the most recent sighting. An All Locations button opens a new window listing all sightings of that species across the region. The same taxonomic ordering, badge bubbles, and filter bar from Notable Sightings apply here too. Requires an eBird API key.

  • Regional Taxonomy report

    Community → Regional Taxonomy generates an interactive species checklist for the currently selected region using live eBird API data. Seen species are marked with a checkmark; unseen species appear in grey. When a media catalog is open, a blue dot marks each species you have photographed. Filter buttons let you focus on seen, unseen, photographed, or not-yet-photographed species. Requires a free eBird API key.

  • eBird API key in Preferences

    A new field in Preferences stores your personal eBird API key, required for community reports. Get a free key at ebird.org/api/keygen.

v1.44 2026
  • Animated First Sightings Map

    A new animated map that plays back the first sighting of each species under the current filter. Unlike the Animated Lifer Map (which always shows life lifers), this map respects your active filter, so you can watch your county firsts, year firsts, or any filtered species list accumulate chronologically.

  • Animated map tooltips

    During playback of either animated map, a tooltip tracks each newly plotted species, showing its name, rank, date, and location.

  • Fix — Animated Sequence Map crash

    Resolved a crash that occurred in the Animated Sequence Map with large photo collections.

v1.41 2026
  • Media catalog safeguards

    A series of fixes prevents accidental data loss when opening, switching, or closing media catalogs. The app now guards against overwriting an existing catalog when a new eBird data file is opened.

  • No catalog, no problem

    Adding photos without a catalog open now prompts you to create one before saving. If you cancel, your in-progress work is preserved and you can try again.

  • Unsaved-changes protection

    Closing the Manage Photos window or the media catalog while changes are pending now asks whether to save or discard, rather than silently discarding.

  • Catalog-switching guard

    Opening a different media catalog while Manage Photos is open is blocked to prevent conflicts. Switching catalogs with unsaved changes prompts to save first.

  • CSV catalog conversion required

    Legacy CSV media catalogs must now be converted to the .jsonl format before they can be used. The app guides you through the conversion and will not open a CSV without it.

  • Default catalog tracking fixed

    Declining "Set as default catalog?" now correctly preserves the previous default. The Preferences dialog always shows the stored default rather than the currently open catalog.

  • Menu visibility improvements

    The Photos menu, Close eBird Data File, and catalog-related File menu items are now shown and hidden based on what is actually open, reducing clutter.

v1.4 2025
  • eBird species code in Rename Photos

    The Species Name Format picker now includes eBird Species Code (e.g. gretit1) alongside Common Name and Scientific Name.

  • Smarter photo-to-species matching

    When adding photos to the catalog, filename matching now checks eBird codes, BBL banding codes, common names, and scientific names as substrings, handling arbitrary filename patterns from any camera or workflow. Previously only whole-word token matching was used.

  • Duration-aware checklist matching

    Photo EXIF timestamps are now matched to the checklist whose window (start time + duration) is closest, so a photo taken mid-checklist correctly matches that checklist rather than the next one to start.

  • Sighting Filter label

    The filter panel is now labelled Sighting Filter to distinguish it from the Photo Filter.

  • Chart names updated

    Photo-related charts are now named Families & Orders by Photos, Total Photos, New Species Photographed Each Year, and Photographed Species Growth Over Time.
